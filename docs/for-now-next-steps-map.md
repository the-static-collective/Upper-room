# Upper Room — For Now Next Steps Map

**Status:** project-local sequencing map / not a roadmap commitment  
**Date:** 2026-08-25

> **Upper Room preserves the thread, not the pose.**

This page records the strongest **for-now growth sequence** visible from Upper Room's current executable state.

It is intentionally not a feature backlog, product promise, or authorization to implement every reachable idea. Its job is to keep the nearby doors visible while preserving the order in which they appear most likely to teach us something without forcing Upper Room to become a different product.

## Current floor

Upper Room has already earned three distinct pieces of ground:

1. **Scripture heartbeat** — a phone-first Scripture surface with stable canonical coordinates and a translation-neutral adapter boundary.
2. **Dust Specimen 001** — attention may become coarse, decaying, non-authoritative weather without becoming command.
3. **Branch / Return Witness v0** — branches may diverge, encounter, coexist, refuse, merge only under separate admission, and later return without counterfeiting uninterrupted presence.

The governing edge is now explicit:

> **continuity without captivity**

That means the next useful expansions should reuse the existing continuity grammar rather than add unrelated application furniture.

## For-now sequence

```text
SCRIPTURE
  |
  v
1. SOVEREIGN PRESENCE
  |
  v
2. TEXTUAL / CONTEXT WITNESS BOUNDARY
  |
  v
3. FEDERATED ROOM ENCOUNTER
  |
  v
4. RESPONSE ECOLOGY
  |
  v

```

The sequence is not irreversible. A later specimen may reorder it. For now, this ordering minimizes architectural guessing.

## 1. Sovereign Presence

**Status:** earned next product primitive

Upper Room is not yet a shared room in executable product terms. The next smallest proof is therefore actual presence without remote control.

Desired shape:

```text
Scripture surface
  +
my sovereign viewport
  +
another participant's visible viewport
  ->
shared attention without possession
```

### Smallest proof

Two participants inhabit one passage.

- each keeps an independent viewport;
- either may look through the other's visible location;
- neither can seize the other's reading state;
- disconnect / reconnect does not manufacture unwitnessed presence;
- durable history remains selective rather than telemetry-heavy.

### Why first

Presence gives selections, AIHYPER, branches, return, and later federation a real human encounter to attach themselves to.

---

## 2. Textual / Context Witness Boundary

**Status:** near; design before implementation

Two nearby doors belong together conceptually but should remain separable in code.

### A. Textual Witness Lens

One stable Scripture coordinate may expose multiple attributable reading witnesses without overwriting one another.

Possible later layers include:

- translation witness;
- source-language form;
- transliteration;
- variant reading;
- lexical / grammatical note;
- uncertainty or lacuna note;
- editorial / reconstruction note.

The invariant is:

> **selection of a witness does not erase neighboring witnesses or silently convert editorial choice into textual authority.**

The existing frontier lives in [issue #4](https://github.com/the-static-collective/Upper-room/issues/4).

### B. Declared Context + AIHYPER

AIHYPER should behave as a bounded door-maker, not a master narrator.

A participant explicitly invokes context. One declared source may then provide bounded testimony with visible provenance and non-authority. AI may propose a relation; it may not back-write that synthesis as Scripture, source testimony, or canonical interpretation.

Smallest useful specimen:

```text
one Scripture selection
  ->
explicit AIHYPER invocation
  ->
one bounded witness packet
  ->
visible source / provenance / non-authority
  ->
proposal
```

### Why before federation

Upper Room should prove locally that testimony can enter a room without stealing the room's authority before it starts exchanging testimony between rooms.

## 3. Federated Room Encounter

**Status:** strongest newly opened architectural door

This is the largest new opportunity revealed by the landed Branch / Return Witness grammar.

Upper Room no longer needs to assume that "shared Scripture" eventually means one synchronized room.

A stronger possibility is:

> **many local rooms can remain themselves, meet, exchange bounded testimony, and preserve what happened without becoming one master room.**

The existing witness grammar already knows how to represent:

- a branch with exact ancestry;
- two branches encountering;
- explicit `coexist`;
- explicit `refuse`;
- `merge` only with separate admission and a distinct descendant identity;
- return without invented continuity.

That is nearly the grammar required for **room-to-room meeting**.

### Smallest proof

```text
ROOM A
  -> Scripture-anchored branch A

ROOM B
  -> Scripture-anchored branch B

A <-> B
  -> one bounded encounter
  -> coexist | refuse | separately admitted bridge
  -> both source histories survive unchanged
  -> both can return home
```

### What this must not become

- a global church graph;
- a master doctrinal merge engine;
- forced consensus;
- one global identity/authority plane;
- automatic synchronization between communities;
- ambient inspection of another room.

### Why it matters

This could let Upper Room scale socially without scaling by centralization.

Families, study groups, churches, friends, classrooms, or temporary gatherings could develop local history and still encounter one another lawfully.

## 4. Response Ecology

**Status:** emerging inference; no current Upper Room feature claim

A Scripture encounter need not produce only commentary.

It may produce a descendant:

- song;
- image;
- voice note;
- testimony;
- question;
- prayer;
- study artifact;
- act of service;
- later creative work;
- another bounded room.

The important architectural move is to treat these as **attributable descendants**, not edits to Scripture and not ambient attachments.

Possible shape:

```text
Scripture selection
  ->
human encounter
  ->
creative / practical response
  ->
attributable branch transformation
  ->
later return to source encounter
```

This creates a porous membrane between Upper Room and the Collective's creative instruments without forcing those instruments into the Upper Room runtime.

### Smallest proof

One Scripture selection produces one external creative response. The response preserves ancestry back to the exact encounter and can later return the participant to that source.

## 5. Scripture-Addressed World

**Status:** horizon / preserved frontier

The camp / Myst / MMO possibility remains strong, but the new continuity work changes how it should be understood.

Do **not** make Upper Room into a game.

Instead, let a future world renderer consume already-proven primitives:

```text
Scripture address
  -> room / branch / encounter
  -> addressable place
  -> traversal / choice
  -> durable witness
  -> changed projected world
  -> return to Scripture
```

The world becomes a **projection over the room ecology**, not the architecture beneath it.

The existing frontier remains [issue #1](https://github.com/the-static-collective/Upper-room/issues/1).

### Smallest eventual experiment

One Scripture selection -> one addressable campfire or cabin -> one human traversal or choice -> one durable witness -> clean return to the passage.

Ordinary 2D web first.

No avatars, persistent currency, VR, open world, or MMO infrastructure are needed to test the primitive.

## Governing constraints

Every future slice should still answer:

1. **What continuity does this preserve?**
2. **What may legitimately be forgotten?**
3. **What is the source identity / anchor?**
4. **Can later state remain answerable to earlier state without rewriting it?**
5. **Can differentiation occur without severing lineage?**
6. **Does participant sovereignty survive the feature?**
7. **Can absence, refusal, or uncertainty remain visible?**
8. **Can someone re-enter without requiring a flattened authoritative summary?**
9. **Does projection remain visibly different from Scripture and source evidence?**
10. **Is this still Upper Room, or are we accidentally building another product inside it?**

## Compact decision rule

When choosing between possible next slices:

> Prefer the smallest experiment that makes a real human Scripture encounter more shareable, attributable, and re-enterable **without increasing captivity**.

If a proposed feature increases synchronization, recording, authority, or platform complexity faster than it increases honest continuity, keep it outside the room.

## Current map in one breath

> **First let people actually be together. Then prove context can enter without becoming authority. Then let rooms meet without becoming one room. Then let encounters bear creative descendants. Only after that, let the world become spatial.**
